Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who We Are

CVTailor is an AI-powered CV analysis tool built to help job seekers improve their applications. We are operated as an independent project. For questions, contact us at osmanzadran21@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

When you use CVTailor we collect:

Account information — your name, email address and password (stored securely and encrypted)
CV content — the text of your CV and job descriptions you submit for analysis
Analysis history — the results of your CV analyses, saved to your account
Google account data — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google
3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

Provide and improve the CV analysis service
Save your analysis history to your account
Send email verification when you change your email address
Authenticate your identity when you log in
4. AI Processing

Your CV text and job descriptions are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI API for analysis. Anthropic processes this data according to their own privacy policy. We do not store your raw CV files — only the text extracted from them and the analysis results.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties except for the services required to operate CVTailor — Anthropic (AI analysis), Resend (email delivery), and Railway (hosting).

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from your Account Settings page. Deletion is permanent and immediate.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

Access the data we hold about you
Delete your account and all your data
Update your personal information at any time
Contact us with any privacy concerns at osmanzadran21@gmail.com
8. Cookies

CVTailor uses a session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
